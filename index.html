How the game works

Type the text shown in the box. Every run gives you a speed in words per minute (WPM) and an accuracy score. Space starts a new run, Tab restarts the one you are on, and Backspace fixes a mistake.

Modes

  • Quotes and Random Words are normal runs.
  • Timed is a sprint against the clock (30 seconds by default).
  • Adaptive builds short drills from the keys you miss or type slowly.
  • Challenges are daily and weekly goals that pay out XP.

XP, levels and the shop

Finished runs earn XP. Every 500 XP is a level, and each level gives you a crystal to spend in the shop on key-sound packs, music and cosmetic extras.

The story

In Story mode every run is a mining trip. You pilot a ship through the Lyra Gate, break asteroids by typing, and harvest Aether crystals. The whole player base shares one weekly team goal, which you can open from the Team goal button in the top bar.

Accounts

You can play as a guest; progress stays in this browser. Create a free account to keep your scores across devices and appear on the leaderboards.
